# Imagery and icons: Oiê

_Stage 3. Approved by the owner on 2026-11-18._

## Imagery: real photos + shapes from the logo
- **People = real photos.**
  - **Inside the app:** only the family's own contact photos. No stock, no drawn people. A contact with no photo gets their initial on a cream circle.
  - **Website and social:** candid photos of real moments, for example a grandparent laughing at a voice message, hands holding a tablet, or a kitchen table. Warm, natural light; a slightly warm colour grade; plenty of calm space.
- **Decoration = shapes from the logo.** Round, touching forms taken from the "Close" letters (the o's ring, the i's dot and stem, the ê's hat), in coral and cream. Use them in empty states, splash screens, social backgrounds and the website. One cluster of shapes per screen at most, never behind text you must read.
- **Banned:**
  - Stock "happy family" photos: posed, studio, white backgrounds, fake smiles.
  - **Identifiable children's faces in marketing** (website, social, store listing). Show hands, backs, or adults instead.
  - Phones or tablets as hero objects: no glowing screens or device beauty shots.
  - Fear imagery: shadows, strangers, locks, warning signs, radar, eyes.
  - Speech bubbles, hearts, houses, mascots or faces drawn on the logo shapes.
  - Anything green, yellow or pink that echoes WhatsApp or the telecom Oi.

## Icons: one rounded line set, bold
- One line icon set with round caps and joins, at a bold stroke that sits with the logo's round letters.
- **Size:** 24 dp grid; touch target 48 dp.
- **Colour:** ink, or ink-muted for secondary. Coral only on the one primary action of a screen.
- **Function only:** icons label actions and objects; they never decorate. An icon that carries meaning always has a text label (kid side: always).
- **Never emoji as icons.**
- **The set:** chosen in Stage 4 (candidates: Phosphor Bold, Material Symbols Rounded at a heavy weight). Check that it has every icon v1 needs: microphone, camera or photo, sticker, send, contacts, clock or quiet hours, settings, link. Check the licence too.
