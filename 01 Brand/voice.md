# Voice: Oiê

_Stage 3. Approved by the owner on 2026-11-18._

- **Three words:** warm · playful · plain
- **One-line rule:** say it like you'd say it at the table. Plain, spoken Portuguese, nothing a kid or a grandparent wouldn't say.
- **One voice, two registers:**
  - **Kid (9–12):** shorter, lighter, a bit playful. Never baby talk, never tells them off.
  - **Parent:** calm, plain and explaining. It says the risk and the limits straight, without fear words.
- **Sentences:** short. What happened first, then what to do.
- **Address:** "você", informal, for everyone. In English, "you".
- **Languages:** written in **Brazilian Portuguese first**; English is translated from it and checked by a native reader.

## Mechanics
- No exclamation marks. **One exception:** the greeting "Oiê!" itself.
- No emoji in the app's own text. The kid can send emoji and stickers; Oiê's own words don't use them.
- No em dashes.
- Sentence case. Buttons start with a verb: "Mandar oi", "Gravar áudio", "Escolher contatos" / "Say hi", "Record a voice message", "Choose contacts".
- Numbers always with their label: "3 contatos", never a bare "3".
- PT-BR: quotation marks “ ”; thousands separator "."; dates "18 de nov." or 18/11/2026; time 16h30. EN: “ ”, ",", "Nov 18", 4:30 pm.
- Links: strip tracking parameters (`utm_*` and similar) before publishing.

## Never use
- **Hype:** unlock, seamless, leverage, next-generation, game-changer, cutting-edge, revolucionário, incrível.
- **WhatsApp-isms in Oiê's own text:** Zap, Zapzap, Wpp. Write "WhatsApp" only where it's needed to explain how Oiê works. Oiê is not a WhatsApp product.
- **Tech words in the UI:** ponte/bridge, sincronizar/sync, sessão/session, servidor/server, API, "dispositivo vinculado"/linked device. Say what happens instead ("conectar ao seu WhatsApp" / "link to your WhatsApp").
- **Claims (product law):** never say a child is "100% safe" (100% seguro), or that Oiê replaces a parent's attention. Never imply Oiê is made or approved by WhatsApp or Meta.
