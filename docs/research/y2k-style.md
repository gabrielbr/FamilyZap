# Research: the Y2K look (for the landing page, decision 0015)

_2026-11-21, for the owner's request: "research for Y2K graphics and style. Use it for that. Same concept of 90's and storytelling"._

## What it is
- **When:** the late 1990s and early 2000s, roughly 1997–2004. The Y2K aesthetic came after Memphis design and before Frutiger Aero (Wikipedia, CARI).
- **In one line:** CARI calls it "a time when the future was tight leather pants, silver eyeshadow, shiny clothing, oakleys, gradients, and blobby electronics."

## Its parts
| Part | What it looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Materials | Metal and synthetic: "chromecore", sleek whites with metallic chrome | Wikipedia |
| Translucent plastic | Technology "entirely made of translucent casing in vibrant colors", sometimes with glitter. The iMac G3 (1998) set it off; translucent candy colours then spread to every kind of product (Game Boy Color, N64, cameras) | Wikipedia; Macworld; Built In |
| Colour | Bright lime, orange and hot pink, with sleek whites and chrome | Wikipedia |
| Shapes | Blobjects: smooth, rounded, computer-made shapes with shiny, liquid surfaces | Wikipedia; Webflow |
| Type | Chunky or rounded fonts, 3D, glossy or metallic | Wikipedia |
| Surfaces | Liquid chrome lettering, iridescent "CD-ROM" foil, bubbles, sparkles | design guides (Webpeak, Studio 2am) |
| The web of the time | High-gloss gel buttons, silver panels, chrome borders, pop-up windows, chunky pixel type | Webflow; Wix |
| 3D quality | Early, a little primitive. Frutiger Aero, which came later, is higher-definition and nature-themed (bokeh, water, skies) | Wikipedia (Frutiger Aero) |

## What we take for Oiê
- **The object:** the product as a candy-plastic gadget from 1999: a translucent tangerine tablet (the brand's coral, pushed to candy) whose insides show through, with a home-phone handset and a lime curly cord. It's rendered in 3D, not drawn, as the owner asked ("avoid the vector look").
- **Textures, not flat vectors:**
  - film grain over the whole page;
  - holographic foil;
  - liquid chrome (sky, a hard horizon, a warm ground);
  - sparkles on a blueberry night;
  - the iMac's fine inner stripes on the candy colours.
- **Era signs used as story:**
  - "today's" phone is buried in old-web pop-up windows (ads, "Quem é você?");
  - the comparison sits in an old desktop window;
  - the buttons are candy gel.
- **What we leave out:**
  - hot pink as a main colour (one accent family is enough);
  - pixel fonts (Nunito stays the one family);
  - McBling (disco, rhinestones);
  - Frutiger Aero's nature imagery.
