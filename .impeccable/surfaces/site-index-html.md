# Surface: the Oiê landing page (site/index.html), v3 Y2K

- **Mode:** Persuade.
- **Audience:** parents of kids 9–12, Brazil first, mostly on phones. A friend's feedback: v2 felt like monitoring kids.
- **Action:** "Baixe já", the latest APK, free.
- **Constraints:** PT-BR first, with EN. Decision 0015 (exceptions for this page only): "!", Nunito 900/italic, a Y2K palette, gradients/gloss/chrome, gel pills. Product law: the risk is readable next to the download, no "100% safe", no WhatsApp logo, no invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The home phone of the 90s, sold back to kids in the look of 1999. The page is a scroll-told story, one beat per scene:
1. hook;
2. today's phone;
3. "Apresentamos o Oiê";
4. 1-2-3;
5. "tem mais";
6. the comparison;
7. the price;
8. "baixe já".

It refuses the monitoring-app page (dashboards, parent controls up front) and flat, drawn vector illustration.

OWN-WORLD: The world is Y2K (CARI, Wikipedia; research in docs/research/y2k-style.md).
- **The product:** a candy translucent plastic gadget (tangerine, its guts showing through, iMac stripes).
- **Surfaces:** liquid chrome (a sky, a hard horizon, a warm ground) for the big lettering and the seams; holographic foil; a blueberry night with four-point sparkles; brushed silver; candy lime and tangerine.
- **Over everything:** film grain.
- **Objects:** gel pill buttons and old-web pop-ups and windows.
- **The art is rendered, never drawn:** three.js renders and NumPy textures (tools/site-art).
- **Type:** Nunito 900 italic, huge, over Nunito body.

STORY: The visitor remembers calling a friend, sees why a smartphone is the wrong tool for a 9-year-old, meets Oiê, sees it's easy and free, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Holographic foil under grain.
- The huge italic headline "Lembra do telefone de casa?".
- On the right (above it on phones): the rendered tangerine tablet with its handset lifted on a lime curly cord. Its screen shows Vó calling, and it rings twice on arrival.
- A tangerine gel button, "Baixe já. É grátis!".
- A "Segurança" link in the nav.
