# Surface: the Oiê landing page (site/index.html)

- **Mode:** Persuade.
- **Audience:** parents of kids aged 9–12 (Brazil first), deciding whether to install Oiê on the kid's tablet. Grandparents and family are the second readers.
- **Action:** "Baixar o Oiê" downloads the latest APK from gabrielbr/oie-releases (no Play Store), with a short how-to-install note.
- **Proof:** the mechanism dramatized (the message's route), the parent's controls, what the family sees, and the honest risk.
- **Constraints:** PT-BR first with an EN switch. Brand rules in `02 Design System/README.md`. No device beauty shots, no bubbles, no green, no identifiable kids, no safety claims, no WhatsApp affiliation. The ban risk is said plainly.
