# Audit · v0.41.0 · the low-risk pass

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **What changed:**
  - `SendPace` is now 4 s and 20 per 10 min (was 2 s and 30);
  - `AppState.markUnreadOn` (saved, off by default), which `RealLink` checks before `markUnread`;
  - a new "Sua conversa com {kid}" section in Ajustes e ajuda, with one switch, shown only once the parent's line exists;
  - the parent-line card's "Por quê?" note says where the switch is.
- **The owner's choices (2026-11-21):** 4 s and 20; the unread mark off by default, for existing installs too.
- **Why:** the owner asked whether nchat or wptui would lower the risk to the WhatsApp number. Both use whatsmeow too, so the risk comes from behaviour, and this pass reduces the unusual behaviour.
- **Skills used and how:** ux-user-flows (§13), graphic-design-fundamentals (§13) and less-is-more were loaded and read earlier in this session. impeccable's audit dimensions are applied to the one new section, checked in the tablet light/dark screenshots and the phone screenshot at 1.3× text.
