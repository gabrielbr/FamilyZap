# Audit · v0.34 · design pass over every screen

- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Trigger:** the owner asked for every screen to be evaluated and re-evaluated with all the Gabriel boilerplate's design skills until each reaches its maximum score, and for the result to be merged.
- **Skills (all loaded and read this session):**
  - ux-user-flows (§13);
  - impeccable: critique with `critique.md` and `critique-rubric.md`; native audit with `audit.native.md`, scored against `android.md`; `craft-floor.md` read before every UI edit;
  - graphic-design-fundamentals (§13);
  - less-is-more;
  - anti-slop (the playbook's stage 6 order).
- **Method:**
  - Four independent reviewers, one per area: the kid's home, the conversation and its media, setup and linking, and the parent area. Each loaded the five skills and every screenshot of its area, checked findings against the source, and scored.
  - In each round I fixed, re-recorded and looked again; the reviewers then re-scored.
  - A fifth reviewer, new to the app, then reviewed the whole app for consistency across screens, twice.
- **Screens:** every tablet screenshot (light and dark), every phone one (360dp at 1.3× text), and new screenshots for every screen that had none:
  - welcome, quiet-hours step, done, period editor, connection;
  - every state of linking (QR, waiting, failed, offline, already linked);
  - forgot-PIN (explanation, QR, new PIN);
  - offline home, recording, its last 10 seconds, the slip line;
  - a long chat that opens on "Novas";
  - setup in dark.

## What the pass found and fixed (the main ones)

### P0
- **"Esqueci o PIN" skipped the proof.**
  - It went to setup step 2, which jumped ahead when WhatsApp was already linked, and then wiped the kid's contacts and chats: anyone holding the tablet could set a new PIN and pick contacts.
  - Now `ForgotPin` logs the tablet out of WhatsApp and shows a fresh QR, and only a QR shown on that screen, this visit, counts.
  - Then:
    - the same WhatsApp: a new PIN, with nothing else lost;
    - another account: setup starts over;
    - an unreadable account: nothing is undone and the parent tries again.

### P1
- **The kid's home didn't scroll:** people past the first rows were unreachable on a phone.
- **TalkBack could reach the screen behind the notice to the kid.**
- **Back in the period editor** left the parent area and lost the period.
- **The 2-minute lock ignored typing and scrolling.**
- **The undo snackbar sat off screen on a phone.**
- **The chat opened at the oldest message.** It now opens on "Novas", or on the newest.
- **In dark mode, sheets brightened the screen.** There is now a `scrim` token per theme.
- **Setup's Back bounced forward.**
- **Linking had no progress, no way out and no error lines.**
- **Fields:**
  - unlabelled for TalkBack;
  - a border below 3:1;
  - errors not announced.

### Shared components (one fix, every screen)
- `pressable` (the 97% press) on buttons, tiles, links and keys.
- QuietButton is 52dp.
- TextLink aligns with the text around it, wraps, and is as tall as a button.
- New components:
  - `SwitchRow` (the whole row toggles);
  - `BottomSheet` (Back, scrim, drag down);
  - `Snackbar` (one action, announced);
  - `StopButton` (the destructive fill);
  - `Rounds` (the brand's touching rounds);
  - `Field` with `error`, `keyboard` and `names`.
- One side margin (README §6) shared by the bar and the content.
- Pictures, avatars and video frames load off the main thread; frames are cached.

### Copy
- One verb on the kid's side: **Mandar**.
- "Com quem … pode falar" instead of "chamar" (there are no calls).
- Curly quotes.
- Help names the right button.
- Shorter reminder lines.
- "Termina em 5 s" in the last 10 seconds of a recording.
