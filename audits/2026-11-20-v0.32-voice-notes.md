# Audit · v0.32 · voice messages play for the family

- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Trigger:** the owner: the kid records and listens in Oiê, but the family receives an empty or broken audio.
- **Process:** the owner's rule.

    - non-Opus input refused.
  - Audit with the four skills (loaded and read this session); docs; ship.
- **Screens:** none change (screenshots pass unchanged).

## Cause and fix
- **Cause:** Android records Opus in Ogg with its own Ogg writer. Android's player accepts that file; the owner's test shows WhatsApp didn't.
- **Fix:** Oiê now re-packs every Ogg recording before sending, into the same shape WhatsApp's own voice notes have. The audio itself (the Opus packets) is untouched; the headers, page positions and checksums are rewritten, and the length is computed from the audio.
- **Unreadable recordings:** a recording that isn't Ogg Opus is refused and shows "Não enviada", instead of reaching the family broken.
- **The report** logs each voice send's format and size, with no audio.
- **Open:** the owner's device test confirms the family can play them. If not, the report now says what was sent.

## ux-user-flows
- **#9 errors:** a broken message reaching the family was a silent failure: the kid thought it had worked. A refused one now shows as not sent, with Tentar de novo.
