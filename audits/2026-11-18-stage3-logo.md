## 3. graphic-design-fundamentals (§13 critique)
| # | Item | Pass? | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brief | ✅ | A wordmark, warm, not babyish, not a bubble, heart, house or face, as the owner asked. It works as the tablet app icon. |
| 2 | Concept | ✅ | One idea: round letters that touch, like a family holding on. The circumflex makes it Oiê, not "oi". |
| 3 | Hierarchy | ✅ | Squint test: the "oi" pair reads first, then the ê with its hat. |
| 4 | Contrast | ✅ | A heavy, even weight; the counters are open enough to read. |
| 5 | Alignment and grid | ✅ | Built on a 256 grid: x-height 93–207, one stroke weight (32), touching joins overlap by 4 units. The circumflex is centred on the e. |
| 6 | Spacing | ✅ | Letters touch by design. Clear space is set (the i's dot diameter). |
| 7 | Typography | ✅ | Drawn letters, no font, so there is no licence issue. The type family comes in Stage 4. |
| 8 | Colour | ✅ | Works in greyscale and in one colour. Coral is 3.6:1 on white and 4.8:1 on ink (graphics need 3:1). Coral is not for small text. |
| 9 | Consistency | ✅ | The same stroke weight and round ends throughout; the symbol is cut from the wordmark. |
| 10 | Simplicity | ✅ | Three letters, one hat, nothing to remove. |
| 11 | Real-size test | ✅ | Wordmark checked at 64/48/32/24/16 px, app icon at 64/32/16 px. A small-size cut exists for 24 px and below. |
| 12 | Accessibility | ✅ | The logo carries no meaning by colour. In UI the name is also text ("Oiê"). |
| 13 | Production | 🟡 | Masters are filled-outline SVG with no strokes or text; PNG and ICO exported. Print values (CMYK, Pantone) and PDF/EPS are not made yet. |
