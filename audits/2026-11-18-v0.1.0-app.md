## Addendum · photo sending and saving on the tablet, 2026-11-18
- **ux-user-flows** ("Kid sends a message", step 3c and branches):
  - Camera or Galeria, the last one first (Remembers).
  - A preview with Trocar and Enviar; back drops the photo; undo returns to the preview.
  - A photo that can't be read shows "Não deu para abrir essa foto. Tente outra."
  - The permission branch from the flow doesn't arise: the system photo picker and the camera app need no permission from Oiê.
  - Setup resumes at the same step after closing (flow "Parent setup + link": "returning mid-setup").
- **impeccable:** photos are downscaled before display (no full-size decode); state saves happen off the main thread. Accessibility: the photo button, the sheet options and the preview image are labelled. Score unchanged (15/20); A-2 is fixed.
- **Privacy (product law):**
  - State is sealed with AES-GCM under a key kept in the Android Keystore.
  - Photos live in the app's private storage and are re-encoded, which removes location metadata.
  - The FileProvider shares only the camera's one temporary file.
  - Backup is off (`allowBackup=false`), so nothing leaves through cloud backup.
  - **Open:** encrypt the photo files themselves (P3).
- **graphic-design-fundamentals:** the photo in the chat sits in the kid's soft-coral bubble, with "Enviada" under the latest message on the right.
- **less-is-more:** the source sheet has two choices and no extra text; no confirm before sending a photo (undo instead).
- **Fixed on the first look:**
  - "Enviada" was on the left, now under the message;
  - the screenshots used the real clock, so CI failed on them (now a pinned clock and time zone);
  - the camera link was built before it was needed, which crashed in tests (now built on tap).

## Addendum · v0.3 (self-update, release workflow, portrait), 2026-11-18
