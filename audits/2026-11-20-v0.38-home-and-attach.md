## graphic-design-fundamentals (§13)
- **Hierarchy:** name first (title on the card), then the last message, then the actions. Unread lifts the message to bold ink with the "novas" pill; read ones sit in `muted`.
- **Accent discipline:** the home has no coral fill; the chat's record button stays the one accent.
- **Contrast:** `muted` on `surface` and `page`, ink on `selected`, page on ink (the pill), `coral-text` on `surface`: all checked pairs, both themes.
- **Grid and spacing:** the side margin; the card gap, padding and inner gaps from the 4/8 scale; the preview starts on the photo's edge (one axis).
- **Type:** Nunito roles only (title, label, body, body-small, caption).
- **Grayscale:** unread reads by weight and the pill, never by colour.
