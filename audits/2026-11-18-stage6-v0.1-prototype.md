### Critique (Assessment A, in-context; native platform, so no Assessment B)
- **Design specificity:**
  - **Kid home:** after adding the brand's touching rounds and real photos, it belongs to Oiê. Before that it was generic.
  - **Conversation:** specific through the kid/family colour split and the big coral record button, with no WhatsApp look.
- **Cognitive load:** 0 failures on the kid side. The parent area is chunked into 4 sections.
- **Nielsen heuristics (0–4):** status 3 · real world 4 · control 3 · consistency 4 · error prevention 3 · recognition 4 · flexibility 3 · minimalist 4 · error recovery 3 (error states not drawn) · help 2 (Ajuda not drawn).
- **Personas:**
  - **Jordan (first-time parent):** the risk notice is clear, and the link steps are concrete. Risk: can't find "Dispositivos conectados".
  - **Casey (distracted):** setup resumes where it stopped.
  - **Sam (screen reader):** the record button needs a label that follows its state.
  - **The kid, 9–12 (project persona):** big faces, one tap to a person, nothing to read. Risk: the empty space under few contacts.

## 5. anti-slop
- No card sprawl: the bubbles are functional, and there are no card grids.
- No eyebrows, no decorative pills (the unread pills carry live counts).
- No fake metadata ("Passo 2 de 6" and the times are real).
- No emoji, no banned words, no em dashes in UI text.
- Sample names and the QR code are labelled as examples.

## What was cut
The permanent record hint, the repeated red links, the coral decoration dot, the per-message "Enviada", a setup tutorial, the parent app, and the sync server.

The same five skills, applied to the 13 new screens and the 6 stickers.

  - the undo snackbar carries one action;
