# Audit · v0.49.0 · one small animation for every action

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **The owner:** "Make that every action of the user has one small interaction animation."
- **Inventory first:**
  - Before, 19 touch targets used `pressable` (the 97% press).
  - 23 more didn't:
    - the parent area's tabs, rows, add rows and period rows, the Nomes row and the help rows;
    - Arrumar's view chips and heart toggle;
    - the reaction emojis, colour swatches and chips;
    - photo tiles, sticker tiles, the media in a chat and the message bubbles;
    - the period editor's days, the setup contact rows, the switch rows and the error note.
- **What changed:**
  - `Components.kt` gets `pressScale`, `pressableSelect`, `pressableToggle`, `pressableCombined`, `pressScaleOnHold` and `motionColor`. All use the 150ms standard and the one ease, and none move with "Remove animations".
  - Every one of the 23 targets now uses them. Picked fills and outlines fade in 150ms.
  - Help answers open with `animateContentSize`, and the chevron turns.
  - Chat items use `animateItem` (fade and placement).
- **Skills used and how:** all four were loaded and read in this session.
  - `ux-user-flows` wrote `docs/flows/interaction-motion.md`.
  - `impeccable` (native audit) was scored against `reference/android.md` and the craft floor's motion check ("one authored moment, exponential ease-out from an already-visible default").
  - `graphic-design-fundamentals` §11 (UI motion 150–300ms, purposeful, reduced motion respected) and `less-is-more` were applied.
  - The screenshots were re-recorded: at rest, only three changed, by 1-pixel anti-aliasing (measured: no pixel differs by more than 40/255).

## 1. ux-user-flows (§13)
- **Speed ✅:** the press shows within 0.1 s, and taps still act on release, so nothing waits for an animation.
- **Accessibility ✅:** "Remove animations" gives no movement, but the picked colour still changes. The semantics (`Role.Tab`, `RadioButton`, `Checkbox`, `Switch`, selected state) are unchanged.
- **Ethics ✅:** motion only answers the person's own touch; nothing moves to grab attention.
