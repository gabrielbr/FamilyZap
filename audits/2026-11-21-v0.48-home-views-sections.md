## 1. ux-user-flows (§13)
- **Happy path ✅:** opening a chat is one tap in every view.
- **Control ✅:** the view changes back with one tap in Arrumar.
- **Memory ✅:** the choice is remembered.
- **Conventions ✅:** list, grid and mosaic.
- **Accessibility ✅:** the view choice is a radio group with 48dp targets. "Pessoas" and "Grupos" are headings. The mosaic reads left to right, row by row (`traversalIndex`).
- **Evidence 🟡:** which view Tito picks.

## 3. graphic-design-fundamentals (§13)
- **Hierarchy ✅:** each section title is the title style, then its people. In Fotos the face leads, then the name, then the tag.
- **Proximity ✅:** a section's title sits closer to its people (12dp) than to the section before (24dp).
- **Consistency ✅:** the same coral news edge and tag in all three views. The tiles and cards share the surface and hairline.
- **Colour ✅:** the picked view is shown by `selected` and an outline, and announced as selected.
