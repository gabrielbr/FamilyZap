# Audit · v0.14 · photo crash and logout: problem report, crash safety, re-link

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Trigger:** the owner's real test. Text messages worked, but sending a photo closed Oiê, and the tablet ended up logged out of WhatsApp.
- **Process:** the owner's standing rule (AGENTS.md, "Never rush"):
  - design: the new flow `docs/flows/problem-report.md`;
  - develop, with tests;
  - audit: the four skills, on tablet and phone screenshots;
  - fix, docs, ship.
- **Skills:** ux-user-flows and less-is-more (loaded as skills in this session); impeccable `reference/audit.native.md` and `android.md`, and graphic-design-fundamentals §13 (read from the files).

## What was found about the crash
- **Probe:** a photo send on a link that isn't connected returns an error; nothing panics. So the crash happens only on a device, and nothing on the tablet recorded it. Guessing at the cause would break the owner's rule, so v0.14 makes the next occurrence visible and harmless:
  - **Problem report:** what the link did, the WhatsApp library's warnings and errors, each send's result, and any crash with its stack trace, kept on the tablet.
  - **Panic safety:** every entry point into the link library turns a panic into an error (`guard`, tested), so the library can't close the app.
- **A real bug found while reading:** after WhatsApp logs the tablet out, "Conectar o WhatsApp" reused the stopped connection, so linking again failed until Oiê restarted. **Fixed:** a logout drops that connection, and the next link starts fresh with a new QR.
- **Precaution:** photos now go with their width, height and a 72px preview, as WhatsApp's own apps send them (`jpegInfo`, tested). A bare photo could look unusual to WhatsApp, though there's no evidence it caused the logout.
- **Caught before release:** the Android library build rejects exported Go functions with three results. The photo helper is now internal. The full check failed loudly here, and nothing broke on the device.

## ux-user-flows
- **New flow:** `problem-report.md` (actor, job story, entry, states, §13). Two taps to copy.
- **Wayfinding:**
  - after a crash, the report leads Ajustes e ajuda instead of sitting under eight help questions;
  - Conexão points to it ("O Oiê fechou sozinho há pouco." with Ver relatório), because a parent who saw Oiê close looks there first.
- **Control:**
  - the parent chooses where the report goes;
  - Apagar has no confirmation, because nothing personal is lost.
- **Fixed during the audit:** on a phone, the parent area's open tab could sit scrolled off the edge, so you couldn't tell where you were. The tab row now keeps the open section in view (LazyRow, scrolls to it).

**Findings:**
- [P1 → fixed] Re-linking after a logout failed until a restart.
- [P1 → fixed] On a phone, the open parent tab could be off-screen.
- [P2 → fixed] At large text on a phone, Apagar relatório was cut off.
- [P2 → fixed] The report was buried at the end of Ajustes e ajuda after a crash.
- [P3 open] The report's header names the tablet model. It's useful for finding the cause and isn't personal data, so the privacy line ("no messages or names") stays true.

## graphic-design-fundamentals §13
- **Hierarchy:** after a crash, Problemas is the first title, and the crash line is body text in ink, not an alarm colour (calm, and colour isn't used).
- **Consistency:** a quiet button plus a text link, the same pair as elsewhere. No new colours or components.
- **Real size:** checked at tablet 1280×800, and at phone 360dp normal and 1.3×.

## Privacy and product law
- **What the report holds:** link states, send kinds and results, the library's warnings and errors, and crash traces.
- **What it never holds:** message text or names. Any 6+ digit run is masked (tested).
- **Where it lives:** in no-backup private storage; Oiê never sends it.
