# Audit · v0.39.0 · the kid's name on every kind of message

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **What changed:**
  - before a voice note or a family sticker (WhatsApp gives neither a caption), the kid's name goes on its own line (😎**Tito**), once per burst of 2 minutes per chat;
  - `NameLine` (logic, tested) and `RealLink.deliver`;
  - no screen changed on the tablet.
- **The owner's choices (2026-11-21):** once per burst, not before every one; reactions stay bare.
- **Skills used and how:** ux-user-flows (§13), graphic-design-fundamentals (§13) and less-is-more (the five questions, the output) were loaded and read in this session. The impeccable audit is applied to what changed, which is only what the family sees in WhatsApp; no Oiê screen changed, so there's nothing new to capture or critique in the screenshot tests.
