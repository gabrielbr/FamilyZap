## How
- **Android's conversation bubbles:** each chat's notification gets a long-lived shortcut, the contact as a `Person` with their picture (or their letter on the brand's blush), `MessagingStyle`, and bubble metadata.
- The kid's own send re-posts that chat's bubble silently (suppressed notification).
- The window is `BubbleActivity` with the chat's id: it shows only that `Conversation`, independent of the full app's screen. Back finishes the window, folding it into the bubble.
- The field asks for focus when the window opens, so Android brings the keyboard up.
- Chats paused or removed lose their bubbles when Oiê opens (`ChatBubbles.stale`).

## ux-user-flows
- **Happy path:** a bubble, then type. One tap to answer, from any app.
- **Control:** Back folds the window; Android's X removes a bubble; the setting turns all of them off.
- **Product law:**
  - a chat's window shows only that allowed chat, with no way to other chats or the parent area;
  - a paused or removed chat loses its bubble, and an open window closes.
