# Audit · v0.17 · bringing in the contacts after linking

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Trigger:** the owner said the contacts step right after linking listed only groups, and asked for a sync view or status.
- **Process:** the owner's rule.
  - Design first: `docs/flows/parent-setup-link.md`, section "v0.17: bringing in the contacts", with a Mermaid flow and a state table.
  - Then develop, with tests; audit with the four skills (ux-user-flows and less-is-more loaded as skills this session, impeccable `audit.native.md` and `android.md`, graphic-design-fundamentals §13); fix; docs; ship.
- **Screens:**
  - tablet: 20b (setup, syncing), 20c (setup, slow), 20d (Contatos, syncing, dark), 18 (Contatos);
  - phone: p13 (setup, syncing, 1.3× text), p05, p08, p09.

## Root cause and design
WhatsApp sends a new linked device the contact list as a snapshot, seconds to a minute after the link. Groups come at once from another call. So step 5 could show only groups, with nothing saying more was coming (heuristic 1, visibility of system status).
- **The link reports when the list is complete:** `contactsSynced`, on `AppStateSyncComplete` for the contact patch, or after its own one-time fetch.
- **`ContactSync.state`** decides between syncing, ready, slow (after 1 min with no people), empty and offline. It has 8 tests.
- **One line, shared by setup and Contatos:**
  - "Trazendo seus contatos do WhatsApp… N até agora", with a spinner that honours Remove animations;
  - then the list, people first.
  - **Slow:** "Seus contatos estão demorando…", with the add-by-number form open.
  - **Empty:** "Não achamos contatos…", with the form open.

## ux-user-flows
- **Never a dead wait:** the parent can search, add by number or tap Depois at any time.
- **Peak-end:** the first minute after linking used to be confusing; it now says what's happening.
- **Prevention:** a contact added by number in setup is picked automatically and carried into the allowed list.
- **Found and fixed during the audit:**
  - [P1] On a phone at 1.3× text, setup's Continuar and Depois were pushed off the screen: the page didn't scroll and the list had a fixed 300dp height. Now every setup step scrolls, and the list is plain rows.
  - [P2] The fixed-height list left a gap and pushed the add form below the fold. Fixed by the same change.
  - [P2] On a phone the group note was squeezed beside the name. It now sits under the name, in setup and in Contatos.
  - [P3] The selected-contact tick's TalkBack label was a literal "Escolhido". It's now translated (`tx.chosen`).

## graphic-design-fundamentals §13
- **Hierarchy:** title, then the status line (muted while syncing, ink when slow or empty), then the list.
- **Alignment:** the status line aligns with the list's left edge.
- **Consistency:** the same line and form in setup and Contatos. No new colours.
