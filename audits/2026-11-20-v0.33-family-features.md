## What was built
1. **Receipts:** under the kid's latest message, "Enviada", then "Entregue", then "Vovó viu"; in a group, "Alguém viu". A receipt only ever moves forward.
2. **Reactions:**
   - the family's reactions show as chips under the message;
   - the kid reacts with a long press, which opens six emoji and Ouvir;
   - the same emoji again takes the reaction away.
3. **Birthdays:**
   - day and month in the contact's editor, no year;
   - on the day, one line at the top of the kid's home.
4. **The gentle nudge:**
   - off by default, with a switch in Ajustes e ajuda;
   - one person at a time, after 7 days without the kid writing to them;
   - "Agora não" hides it for a week.
5. **Desenhar:** a pad with five palette colours (new product tokens `draw-*`), thin or thick, Desfazer and Limpar. Pronto goes to the photo preview.
6. **Criar figurinha:** a photo's centre, cut round, as a 512 px WebP, kept with the family stickers.
7. **Ouvir:** Android's own text-to-speech on the tablet.
8. **Listen before sending:** Ouvir in the 5-second undo bar. While it plays, the 5 seconds wait.
9. **Preciso de você:**
   - a quiet button on the kid's home, shown only when someone is marked;
   - it asks once, then sends "Preciso de você." to each marked person, at most once every 5 minutes;
   - it goes in quiet hours too.
10. **Fotos:** in the chat header, a grid of that chat's photos, videos and GIFs; tap one to open it big.

### Findings
- **P1 (fixed):** on a phone, the sixth reaction was cut off. The row now shares the width.
- **P1 (fixed):** on a phone at 1.3× text, Ouvir pushed Desfazer off the undo bar. The line now gives way (ellipsis), so Desfazer always shows.
- **P2 (fixed):** the nudge's "Agora não" beside the line squeezed it to 5 lines on a phone. It now sits under the card.
- **P2 (fixed):** the drawing pad's title was cut off on a phone. It now shows the name alone there.
- **P2 (fixed):** the Fotos tiles said "Mandou um vídeo", a notification phrase. They now say "Vídeo" and "GIF".
- **P2 (fixed):** the editor was titled "Mudar nome" though it now holds a birthday and a switch. It is now "Editar contato" (groups keep "Mudar nome").
- **P3 (open):** the "Desenhar" button has no icon. The core line set has no pencil, and the label carries the meaning.

## graphic-design-fundamentals §13
- **Hierarchy:** the home reads: name, then today's birthday, then the people, then the quiet nudge and the button.
- **Accent:** one filled coral action per screen: Pronto on the pad, Mandar in the dialog, Salvar in the editor. Reactions and toggles use `selected`.
- **Colour:** the yellow swatch (`draw-sun`) is 2.1:1 on white paper. It's a drawing colour, not UI. Its swatch has a hairline, a name and an ink check, so it is never colour alone.
- **Consistency:** reminders are cards with the avatar (the radius-8 card), as elsewhere; chips use the pill radius.
- **Real size:** checked at 360dp with 1.3× text, and on the 1280×800 tablet.
