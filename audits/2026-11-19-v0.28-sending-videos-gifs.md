## ux-user-flows
- **Happy path:** camera button, Gravar vídeo or Galeria, wait for "Preparando o vídeo…", then Enviar. Undo for 5 s, as for photos.
- **Errors in words, with a way forward (Trocar):** longer than 3 minutes (checked before any work), too big after shrinking, unreadable.
- **Progress (#1):** a bar while shrinking. Back cancels the work, and nothing is kept.
- **Product law:**
  - no GIF search service: nothing about the kid leaves the tablet;
  - the camera's temporary file and the work files are deleted;
  - quiet hours, the allowed list, pace and retry apply as for every send.
