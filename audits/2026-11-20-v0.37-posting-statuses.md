## ux-user-flows (§13)
The review is in the flow file. In short:
- **Happy path:** Postar, a photo, Postar (three taps plus the camera).
- **"Quem vê":** shown before the choice, under the text field, and on the kid's own status.
- **Branches:** nobody can see it, quiet hours, link down, a video over a minute, an unreadable photo, a failed post (Tentar de novo), 24 hours, switched off.
- **Control:** Trocar and Back before posting; Apagar para todos after; the parent can delete from Meu status.
- **Ethics:**
  - only Contatos sees it, signed with the kid's name;
  - no view counts;
  - off until the parent turns it on;
  - the parent sees every post on their phone.

## graphic-design-fundamentals (§13)
- **Hierarchy:**
  - on the home, Novidades stays smaller than the people;
