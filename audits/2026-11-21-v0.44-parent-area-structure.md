# Audit · v0.44.0 · the parent area split by job (Ajustes and Ajuda)

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **The owner:** "in the parent area, settings is mixed with help and everything is crowded there. Do a careful analysis on how to break down the parent section … use your skills."
- **What changed:**
  - `Parent.kt`:
    - the tabs are Contatos, Silêncio, Ajustes (gear) and Ajuda (info);

  - `ux-user-flows` wrote the flow, with its inventory, card sort and tap counts (`docs/flows/parent-area-structure.md`).

  - `graphic-design-fundamentals` §13 and `less-is-more` were applied on the same screenshots.

## 3. graphic-design-fundamentals (§13)
- **Hierarchy ✅:** in Ajustes, Conexão comes first (its state in one line, the action under it), then the names, then the three switches, each with its title and short help.
- **Proximity ✅:** the unread mark sits with the connection, because both are about the parent's WhatsApp.
- **Consistency ✅:** the Nomes row uses the same chevron as the help rows. Its screen uses the same back and title pattern as the period editor.
