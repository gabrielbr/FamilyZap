# Audit · v0.52.0 · the whole app on Material 3 Expressive

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **The owner:**
  - "I'm inclined on the Material UI";
  - "Just connect to the material design repo";
  - "Go ahead with the implementation keeping defaults, but we want the frame around it. I want you to build everything in a single release. So you keep working and auditing until all screens are done."
- **What changed:**
  - **Toolchain:** Material 3 1.5 (Compose BOM 2026.11.01), AGP 9.2 with built-in Kotlin, Gradle 9.4, Kotlin 2.3, compileSdk 37.1 (targetSdk 35 unchanged). CI installs platform 37.1.
  - **Theme:** `MaterialExpressiveTheme`:
    - the scheme generated from the coral by `design/m3/scheme.py`;
    - Nunito in Material's type scale (600 standing in for 500);
    - Material's shapes;
    - the expressive motion scheme.
  - **Older screens:** their colour names map onto the scheme (`palette()`), so every screen wears it. The content colour is set to on-surface.
  - **Shared components:** Material's buttons, icon buttons, text button, switch, snackbar, sheet look, top-bar height, banner and cookie-shaped faces. Custom presses run on Material's fast spring.
  - **Screens:**
    - Kid home: segmented list, card colours, badge, connected button group, toggles.
    - Chat: joined runs, chip, pill input, FAB-look record button, primary sender names.
    - Parent area: navigation rail and bar.
    - Setup: Material text fields.
    - Statuses: the section subheader.
    - Every border around a card is gone.
  - **Docs:** decision 0018, AGENTS.md, the design system README (§4, §5, §6, §10), `design/README.md`, the flows (kid home, chat, parent area), README, CHANGELOG, STATUS, roadmap.
- **Skills used and how:** all four (ux-user-flows, impeccable with `reference/audit.native.md` against `reference/android.md`, graphic-design-fundamentals, less-is-more) were loaded and read in this session.
  - ux-user-flows updated the three flows: no step changes.
  - The other three were run on every screenshot: 149 tablet (landscape and portrait, light and dark, PT and EN) and 66 phone (360dp, including 1.3× text). They were reviewed on contact sheets, then again after each round of fixes.
  - The comparison mock-up came first (today and Expressive, side by side), shown to the owner before deciding.

## 3. graphic-design-fundamentals (§13)
- **Hierarchy ✅:** the greeting (display), then the primary section titles, then the names. News is the warmer container with one solid badge.
- **Colour ✅:** one generated scheme. Coral is the primary and the frame. Material's tertiary (teal) is used nowhere. One filled primary button per screen.
- **Consistency ✅:** the same shapes, containers and buttons on every screen. Every face is the same shape.
- **Contrast ✅:** checked by the script in both themes, and on the screenshots.
