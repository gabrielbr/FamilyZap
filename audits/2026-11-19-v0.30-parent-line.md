## ux-user-flows
- **The card says three things before the tap:** what's created (a group with only you, named Tito), what flows (both ways), and WhatsApp's limit (no notification for your own account's messages; pin the chat).
- **Branches:** the group refused (the fallback, said in words), not connected (said, nothing created), removed (the card comes back), commands work there.
- **Control:** nothing is created without the tap. Removing uses the usual Remover with undo; the group stays in the parent's WhatsApp, theirs to keep or leave.
- **Ethics:** the notification limit is said up front, not discovered later.
