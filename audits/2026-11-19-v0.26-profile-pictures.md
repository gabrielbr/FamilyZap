## ux-user-flows
- **Recognition over recall (#6):** for a 9–12-year-old, the face is faster than the name, especially for two "V"s (Vovó, Vovô). In 32 the two are told apart at a glance.
- **States:**
  - a picture;
  - none or hidden, or not loaded yet: the letter circle;
  - changed: refreshed within a day.

  Nothing says why a picture is missing: it's the contact's own WhatsApp privacy choice.
- **Product law:**
  - only allowed contacts are looked up;
  - pictures are sealed on the tablet;
  - a removed contact's picture is deleted at the next check.

## graphic-design-fundamentals §13
- **Consistency:** the picture takes exactly the circle's place and size, so the grid doesn't move between a picture and a letter (32).
- **Contrast:** names stay ink on page, below the faces.
- **Colour:** the pictures are the contacts' own; the frame and the coral stay the brand's.
