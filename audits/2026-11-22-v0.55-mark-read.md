# Audit · v0.55.0 · what reaches the kid is marked read on the parent's WhatsApp

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **The owner:** "Messages sent to the kid can be marked as read on the parent's device? So it avoids bombarding the parent with notifications that are not for them." They chose "as soon as it arrives", on by default, with a switch.
- **What changed:**
  - **The link:** `MarkChatRead`, with `readTarget` refusing broadcasts, the parent's own chat, and a group without its sender (`TestReadTarget`). The AAR was rebuilt.
  - **The rule:** `ReadMark.should` (tested): only what reached the kid (notify or quiet), never the parent's line, the parent's or the kid's own messages, a deleted message or an unknown chat; never when off.
  - **The send:** `RealLink` sends the receipt on the background thread right after the message rule.
  - **The setting:** `markReadOn`, on by default, saved (tested, including old saved states).
  - **Ajustes:** "Mensagens para Tito" › "Marcar como lidas no meu WhatsApp", with the honest help line, in PT and EN.
  - **Docs:** the flow `docs/flows/mark-read.md`, decision 0019, rule L7 in `docs/whatsapp-link.md`, CHANGELOG, STATUS.
- **Skills used and how:** the four skills were loaded and read in this session. ux-user-flows wrote the flow first. The other three were run on the changed screenshots: `77-parent-settings` light and dark, `16`, `30`, `54`, and phone `p07`, `p12`.
