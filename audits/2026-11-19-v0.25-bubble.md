## Choices
- **Android's Bubbles, not a home-made overlay.**
  - An overlay needs "draw over other apps", a scary permission that Play reviews.
  - Bubbles are Android's own pattern: drag, the X to dismiss, and folding back on a tap outside all come for free.
  - The bubble window reuses the phone layout Oiê already ships and audits (360dp, 1.3× text).
- **Off by default, turned on by the parent,** behind the PIN: it changes how the tablet behaves.
- **Silent:** the bubble's notification makes no sound and hides behind the bubble.

## ux-user-flows
- **Happy path:** from another app, the bubble then a person, 2 taps. Back, or a tap outside, returns to the other app.
- **#1 status:** the setting says which of these is true:
  - on;
  - Android doesn't allow it yet, with **Permitir bolhas** into Android's own screen, plus the Samsung path on Samsung tablets;
  - notifications are off;
  - the tablet is too old.
  It's re-read when the parent comes back from Android's settings.
- **#3 control:** Android's X, and the switch.
- **Product law:** the window shows the same allowed contacts, and the parent area behind the PIN.
