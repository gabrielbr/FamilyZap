## ux-user-flows
- **States per item:**
  - **video:** downloading ("Vídeo · 0:42"), ready (first frame, ▶, length), too big (in words, no ▶);
  - **GIF:** loading, then a loop;
  - **sticker:** moving, or still with "Remove animations".
- **Control:** videos never play by themselves. The player has ▶/❚❚ and Back; GIFs loop silently, as in WhatsApp.
- **Honour "Remove animations" (AGENTS.md):** stickers and GIFs stay on their first frame.
