# Audit · v0.24 · "Conectando…" instead of a false "desconectado"

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Trigger:** the owner's report: after updating, Oiê shows that it needs to connect again, but it is connected.
- **Process:** the owner's rule.
  - Design first: `docs/flows/parent-setup-link.md`, section v0.24.
  - Develop, with tests: `LinkView`, 4 tests.
  - Audit with the four skills (loaded and read this session); docs; ship.
- **Screens:**
  - new: 29 (kid home while reconnecting: no banner), 30 (Conexão while reconnecting, dark);
  - unchanged: 03 and 16 (a link really down still shows the banner and the button).

## Cause
Every screen read "not up yet" as "disconnected". After an update or a restart, the link takes a few seconds to come back by itself. In that time:
- Conexão said "O Oiê se desconectou" and offered Conectar o WhatsApp;
- the kid's home said "Peça para Ana conectar de novo".

A short network drop that whatsmeow fixes on its own did the same.

## ux-user-flows
| Heuristic | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| #1 Visibility of status | Three states shown as two: "connecting" read as "down". | Connected, Conectando…, Desconectado. Down only after 30 s without coming up. |
| #5 Error prevention | The Connect button invited a needless new link (a new QR) over a working one. | No button while connecting. |
| Kid's experience | A false alarm asked the kid to fetch a parent. | Nothing is said for the first 30 s. The kid can keep writing; messages wait and go out on connect (L4). |

Severity before: important (false alarms on every update, with a risk of needless re-linking).

## impeccable · native audit

- **Timing:** the 30-second grace re-reads once, with no polling loop.
