# Audit · v0.53.0 · pinch to zoom a photo

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **The owner:** "Right now the kid cannot pinch to zoom the images, so they should be able to do it."
- **What changed:**
  - `logic/Zoom.kt` (tested by `ZoomTest`): the scale is held between 1× and 4×, the point under the fingers stays put, and the photo can't be dragged past its edges. Double-tap zooms to 2.5× at the tapped point.
  - `PhotoScreen` uses `ZoomablePhoto`:
    - pinch, drag and double-tap;
    - the TalkBack actions "Ampliar" and "Ver inteira", and the state "Ampliada" while zoomed;
    - the double-tap glides on Material's spatial spring, and jumps at once with "Remove animations".
  - A UI test (`PhotoZoomTest`) covers double-tap, pinch and the TalkBack actions. A new reference image shows a zoomed photo: `84-photo-zoomed`, light and dark.
  - Strings in PT and EN.
  - Docs: the flow `docs/flows/photo-zoom.md`, CHANGELOG, STATUS.
- **Skills used and how:** the four skills were loaded and read in this session. ux-user-flows wrote the flow first. impeccable (`reference/audit.native.md`), graphic-design-fundamentals and less-is-more were run on `84-photo-zoomed`, light and dark.

## 1. ux-user-flows (§13)
The review is in the flow. One-pointer ways exist for everything (double-tap, TalkBack actions), so it meets WCAG 2.5.1.
