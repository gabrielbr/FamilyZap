# Audit · v0.56.0 · Oiê's own intro closed the kid's 24-hour window

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **The owner:** "My kid messaged his cousin in the morning; when they replied at 17:00, he didn't receive the message." The cousin wrote from their own phone, not as a reply. The tablet had linked again during the day.
- **Root cause:**
  - The parent writing in a chat closes the window (v0.40).
  - Oiê's intro (v0.47) is sent a few seconds after the kid's message is timed, and its id lives only in memory.
  - Coming back after a new link or a restart, the intro looked like the parent writing after the kid, so the cousin's answer stayed with the parent.
- **Proof:** `RealInboxTest.oiesOwnIntroIsNotTheParent` failed before the fix ("expected null, but was: the chat"), and passes after.
- **What changed:**
  - `RealInbox.parentWriting` ignores messages that start with `Intro.MARK`.
  - `MessageRule.held` and `AppState.heldReason` give the reason a message stayed with the parent, and `RealLink` writes it to the problem report: one line, the reason only, never the text.
  - Tests: `HeldReasonTest` (3 new) and `RealInboxTest` (1 new).
  - Docs: the flow `docs/flows/kid-receives-messages.md` (v0.56), CHANGELOG, STATUS.
- **Skills used and how:** the four skills were loaded and read in this session. ux-user-flows updated the flow first. There's no screen change, so impeccable, graphic-design-fundamentals and less-is-more were applied to the behaviour and the report line; the screenshots are unchanged (`verifyRoborazziDebug` passes).
