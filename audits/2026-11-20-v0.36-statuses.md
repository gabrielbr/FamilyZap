## ux-user-flows (§13)
The review is in the flow file. In short:
- **Happy path:** tap a face and watch. A reaction is one tap; a reply is two taps and the text.
- **Branches:** off, nobody, arriving, too big, text, deleted while open, remove animations, link down, quiet hours, 24 hours.
- **Control:** Back, Pausar, the left and right halves, a reaction tapped again to remove it.
- **Ethics:** no counts, no streaks, no "seen by". The parent's switch says plainly that a view shows as theirs.
- **Product law:** reactions and replies go to the poster's own chat, never to the status broadcast (that would reach the parent's whole audience). Only allowed, unpaused people; never groups, the parent line or the parent's own statuses; nothing from before the link.

## graphic-design-fundamentals (§13)
- **Hierarchy:** the home keeps the people as the main thing. Novidades is a smaller row (avatars below the people's size), with a small label.
- **Accent discipline:** coral is a ring (a stroke), not a fill. The home's one filled coral thing is unchanged.
- **Contrast:** names and "nova" pass 4.5:1; the seen hairline and the ring pass 3:1, light and dark.
- **Alignment and spacing:** the side margin is shared with the bar; gaps come from the scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24).
- **Type:** Nunito roles only (title for the text status, caption for "há 2 h" and "nova").
- **Real-size test:** checked at 360dp and 1.3× text.
