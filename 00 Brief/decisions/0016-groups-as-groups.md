# 0016 · Groups as groups: the kid sees the whole of an allowed group

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Decided by:** the owner: "a group is a group, so we should not filter this. If a parent allows a kid to be in a group, even though I can see that this can be a security issue, we want to allow the kid to read that … people that are not on their list should have a visual flag that they are not on the list."
- **Before:** an allowed group followed the 1:1 rule. Only messages that started with the kid's name, replies to the kid, or the 24 hours after the kid wrote reached the tablet. Everything else stayed private, unless the parent turned on "Conversa só de {kid}".
- **Decision:**
  - Everything in an allowed group reaches the kid: every member, including people not on the kid's list, and the parent's own messages in that group.
  - Every message carries its sender's name. People not on the list carry "Fora dos contatos".
  - "Conversa só de {kid}" now matters for people only.
- **The product law, reworded** (AGENTS.md, PRODUCT.md): the kid can never reach, or be reached by, a contact the parent didn't allow. The one exception is inside a group the parent allowed, where the kid sees the whole group and outsiders are marked.
- **Why:** a group's conversation only makes sense whole. Filtered, the kid read half-conversations from people they couldn't name. Allowing a group is a decision about that group and its members.
- **What protects the kid:**
  - Only groups the parent allowed reach the kid.
  - The group's editor says, before the parent keeps it, that the kid will see everything from everyone in it.
  - Outsiders are marked in words.
  - The parent can pause or remove the group from the tablet or from their WhatsApp ("/Tito parar 3").
  - Ajuda says it in "Quem pode falar com {kid}?".
- **Revisit if:** a family reports an unwanted adult reaching a kid through a group. Then consider a per-group switch back to the filtered mode.
