# 0014 · The landing page tells the story of the home phone

- **Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Status:** accepted · **By:** the owner
- **Changes:** the words of the landing page (v2, decision 0013). Its design, structure and animations stay as they are. The safety page is unchanged.

## Context
A friend's feedback: the landing page felt like monitoring kids, a "helicopter parent" page. The safety page's story of the 90s home phone (call a friend, make a plan, meet in real life) was more relatable.

Two new looks were tried on the branch first: a 90s TV infomercial, then a Y2K restyle. Neither shipped. The owner: "Stop this implementation. Just update the content of the V2 with the 90's content. Keep the design and structure."

## Decision
The v2 page keeps its hero, family picker, silhouettes, drawn screens, six story steps, FAQ, download and notice. Only the words change, to tell the home-phone story:
- **The hero:** "Lembra do telefone de casa?" The home phone is back, on the tablet, and the meeting happens outside.
- **The steps:**
  - only familiar people on the other end;
  - the message goes out on the family line (the parent's WhatsApp), and the kid knows it from day one;
  - the plan is made and the rest happens away from the screen;
  - the usual crowd, with no strangers, ads or feed;
  - the parent keeps the phone book.
- **The three highlights become "Como o telefone de casa":** the usual crowd, no ads or feed, nothing through us.
- **"Você vê o que sai" leaves the highlights:** it read as monitoring. It stays, honestly, in the FAQ ("Eu vejo o que a criança manda?").
- **The footer adds:** "O Oiê é código aberto. Feito por Gabriel Lopes.", with the code on GitHub.

## Kept
- **Product law:** the WhatsApp risk is said plainly in the notice and the FAQ; no "100% safe"; no WhatsApp logo or affiliation; "o Oiê não tem servidor".
- **The voice:** no "!", no em dashes, and buttons start with a verb.
