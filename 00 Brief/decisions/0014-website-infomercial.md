# 0014 · The landing page becomes a 90s infomercial

- **Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Status:** accepted · **By:** the owner
- **Replaces:** decision 0013's story (the silhouettes and the drawn WhatsApp screens) on the landing page. The safety page is unchanged.

## Context
A friend's feedback: the v2 landing page felt like monitoring kids, a "helicopter parent" page. The safety page's story of the 90s home phone (call a friend, meet in real life) was more relatable.

## Decision
The landing page (v3) is a fun, catchy, slightly goofy 90s TV infomercial. The concept is the home phone: kids talk with family and friends without the risks of a smartphone. It's told as a scrolling story with little text, has the FAQ at the bottom, and closes with "open source, made by Gabriel Lopes", linking to the repo and gabriellopes.com.

**Brand exceptions, approved by the owner for this page only:**
- "!" is allowed in the playful lines;
- Nunito goes to 900 and to italics;
- a 90s TV palette (yellow, teal, purple) joins coral for fields, starbursts and stickers.

The app and the safety page keep every brand rule.

## Kept, whatever the costume
- **Product law:** the WhatsApp risk is said plainly and readably, never in fine print; no "100% safe"; no WhatsApp logo or affiliation; "o Oiê não tem servidor".
- **No invented claims:** no testimonials and no fake phone numbers. "Grátis" and "código aberto" are true.
- **Delivery:** the page asks no other site for anything, and works without motion ("Remove animations").
