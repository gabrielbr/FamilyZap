# 0015 · News on the kid's home in the brand coral, one tag per person

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Decided by:** the owner ("it should be maybe using the red that we use on the brand, and also the notification tag").
- **Context:** the system's rule is one filled brand-coral thing per screen (README §3, AGENTS.md rule 6). On the kid's home, unread messages were shown by a dark "3 novas" tag. The owner found it too soft to catch a kid's eye.
- **Decision:**
  - People with news come first, newest first.
  - Each wears a 2dp brand-coral edge (the new `news-edge` size token) and a coral-filled "N novas" tag with ink on it.
  - Several people with news means several coral tags. This is a named exception to "one filled coral per screen", limited to the news tag on the kid's home.
- **Why it's acceptable:** the tag carries a word (never colour alone), and it marks the thing that needs the kid's attention. The home has no other filled coral action, so nothing competes with it.
