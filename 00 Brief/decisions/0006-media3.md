# 0006 · Media3 to shrink the kid's videos

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Status:** approved
- **Approved by:** Owner (chose "Add Media3, shrink them", 2026-11-19)

## Context
The kid can send videos up to 3 minutes (the owner's choice). A 3-minute video from the tablet's camera is 150–300 MB: too big for WhatsApp to take reliably, and too big to hold in memory to seal and send.

## Decision
- Use Google's **Media3 Transformer** (`androidx.media3:media3-transformer`, Apache License 2.0) to re-encode each video on the tablet to 720p before sending.
- The licence is credited in Ajustes e ajuda › Sobre.
- GIF files are turned into WhatsApp's GIF form (a short silent MP4) with Android's own MediaCodec, since Media3 doesn't read animated GIFs.

## Options considered
| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Send as recorded, up to 64 MB | Only about 30–60 s of camera video would fit; the owner wants 3 minutes. |
| Our own MediaCodec transcoder | Many device quirks (rotation, audio, codecs) that Media3 already handles. |

## Consequences
- The app grows by a few MB.
- Video preparation shows progress ("Preparando o vídeo…").
