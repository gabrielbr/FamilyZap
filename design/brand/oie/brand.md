Source of every value: the owner's approvals of 2026-11-18 (`STATUS.md`). Logo: `01 Brand/logo/`.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| page | `#FFF6EE` cream | `#1F1210` | page background | n/a |
| surface | `#FFFFFF` | `#2A1714` | cards | n/a |

The core names `mint` and `coral` keep their roles: `mint` is "the brand accent", `coral` is
"stop". Under this brand the accent is coral and stop is a deeper brick. Screens use the roles,
never the colour names.

**Type:** Nunito (SIL Open Font License 1.1), weights 400, 600, 700 and 800, with Portuguese accents
(ê, ã, ç, á, õ). It is the only family in the app; the core's Ubuntu Mono is swapped out.
